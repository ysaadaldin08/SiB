Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026 · Applies to the Students in Business co-op placement platform

Students in Business (SiB) is a student co-op placement platform operated within the Ottawa-Carleton District School Board (OCDSB) co-op framework. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information for students, employers, and co-op coordinators.

1. Who we are

Students in Business ("SiB", "we", "our") is a co-op placement platform developed to connect Ottawa-area secondary school students with local businesses for co-op education placements. SiB operates in partnership with OCDSB co-op teachers (coordinators) who oversee all placements.

Questions about this policy can be directed to: ysaadaldin08@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

From students:

  • Full name, school email address, and password (hashed)
  • School name and grade
  • Career track interests and availability (hours per week)
  • Cover notes submitted with co-op applications
  • Résumé files uploaded to the platform
  • Messages exchanged with employers through the on-platform messaging system

From employers:

  • Company name, contact name, and business email address
  • Co-op listing details (role title, description, schedule, location)
  • Messages exchanged with students through the on-platform messaging system

From coordinators:

  • Name, school board email address, and school board affiliation
  • Internal review notes on flagged conversations (never shared with students or employers)

Automatically collected:

  • Account creation timestamps and login activity
  • Application submission dates and status changes
  • Message send timestamps and flagging events

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Match students with co-op opportunities and process applications
  • Facilitate supervised communication between students and employers
  • Allow co-op coordinators to oversee placements and ensure student safety
  • Send email notifications about application status, new listings, and platform activity
  • Enforce our safety policies (content moderation, rate limits, messaging safeguards)
  • Improve the platform and troubleshoot technical issues

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Student data and OCDSB compliance

Students are minors. SiB treats student data with heightened care. All student–employer communications are logged and accessible to registered OCDSB co-op coordinators for safeguarding purposes.

In alignment with OCDSB co-op education policy:

  • Students signing up with an @ocdsb.ca, @ocdsbstudents.ca, or @ocsb.ca email address receive a "Verified school account" badge and are automatically recognised as OCDSB students.
  • Students using other email addresses can still participate, but their accounts are flagged for coordinator review before full platform access is granted.
  • Employers must receive coordinator approval before posting listings, unless their company domain is on the approved KNBA-member list.
  • Messaging is restricted to accepted co-op placements — no cold contact is permitted.

Co-op coordinators can view all message threads, flag conversations, and mark threads as reviewed. Coordinator review notes are internal and never visible to students or employers.

5. Messaging and content moderation

All messages sent through SiB are:

  • Permanently stored and cannot be deleted by either party
  • Automatically scanned for phone numbers, personal email addresses, URLs, social media handles, and off-platform contact phrases
  • Flagged and reviewed by coordinators when suspicious content is detected
  • Subject to a non-dismissable in-app notice reminding both parties that messages are logged and monitored

This logging is a deliberate safeguard to protect students. By using the SiB messaging system, you consent to this monitoring.

6. Data storage and security

Account and application data is stored in Supabase, a cloud database provider with encryption at rest and in transit. Message threads, notifications, and coordinator data are additionally persisted in your browser's localStorage for offline access.

Passwords are hashed before storage and are never stored or transmitted in plain text (with the exception of the current coordinator seed account, which will be replaced with a proper invite flow in a future update).

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no system is completely immune to security risks. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it immediately to ysaadaldin08@gmail.com.

7. Email communications

We send transactional emails for:

  • Email address verification on signup
  • Application status changes (if your preference is set to "Immediate")
  • New co-op listing notifications (if opted in)
  • New message notifications (if opted in)
  • Coordinator alerts for flagged content and new placements

You can update your notification preferences from your dashboard Settings section at any time. You cannot opt out of security-related emails (e.g. email verification).

School board email filters: If you registered with an @ocdsb.ca or @ocdsbstudents.ca address, SiB emails may be filtered by the school board before reaching your inbox. Check your spam folder, or ask your co-op coordinator to manually verify your account.

8. Your rights

You may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account (subject to retention requirements for active co-op placements)
  • Export your data in a portable format

To make a request, email ysaadaldin08@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

9. Cookies and local storage

SiB uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to maintain your login session, cache notification preferences, and store message thread data. This data is stored on your device and not transmitted to third parties.

We do not use tracking pixels, third-party analytics scripts, or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform evolves. Significant changes will be communicated by email or via an in-app notice. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Continued use of SiB after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
