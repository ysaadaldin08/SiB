1. About SiB

Students in Business ("SiB") is a co-op placement platform that connects secondary school students with local employers under the supervision of registered school board co-op teachers (coordinators). The platform is designed to operate within the school board co-op education framework and all placements are subject to coordinator oversight.

2. Eligibility

Students: Must be enrolled in a secondary school co-op program. Accounts registered with a recognised school board email address are automatically recognised as school board accounts. Students using other email addresses are subject to coordinator review.

Employers: Must be a legitimate business or organisation with a genuine co-op opportunity to offer. Employer accounts require coordinator approval before listings can be published, unless the employer's domain is on the approved partner list. By registering, employers confirm that they are able to comply with school board co-op placement requirements, including providing adequate supervision of student placements.

Coordinators: Must be registered school board co-op teachers. Coordinator accounts are created manually — there is no public signup.

5. Messaging and communication

Student safety is the highest priority on this platform. All messages between students and employers are logged, monitored by automated systems, and accessible to registered co-op coordinators.

Messaging on SiB is restricted to employer–student pairs where the student's application has been formally accepted. Cold contact (messaging a student before accepting their application) is not permitted.

Every conversation includes a permanent, non-dismissable notice reminding both parties that messages are logged and reviewed. By sending a message, you consent to this logging and coordinator access.

The automated content scanner checks every message for personal contact information (phone numbers, email addresses, social media handles) and off-platform phrases. If your message triggers a flag, you will be shown a warning before it can be sent. Flagged messages notify all registered coordinators and are prioritised for review.

Persistent misuse of the messaging system — including repeated attempts to move communication off-platform — will result in account suspension.

Employers who fail to meet these standards may have their accounts suspended and listings removed without notice.
